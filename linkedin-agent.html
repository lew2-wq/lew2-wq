Topic 01 · Alumni outreach

What this module explains

Finding the right alumnus is part of the task.

The agent can search for alumni who match a target school, company, or role; compare likely profiles; draft a personalized message; and wait for approval before sending.

Action workflow · Steps 01–05

How the agent completes the task

These are actions in sequence—not separate topics. Each step moves the same task closer to a verified result.

1
Step 01 · Define

Describe the alumni profile

Specify the school, target company or function, and the reason for reaching out.

Find Carnegie Mellon alumni working in product management at [company]. Shortlist the best matches and draft a concise outreach message. Do not send anything until I approve the recipient and message.
2
Step 02 · Search

Find and compare likely alumni

The agent opens LinkedIn in the browser and checks visible signals such as school, role, employer, graduation period, location, and any existing connection.

3
Step 03 · Review

Approve the person and wording

The agent presents the strongest match and a personalized draft. You confirm both before an external message is sent.

4
Step 04 · Execute

Open the correct conversation

After approval, the agent opens the selected profile or thread, enters the reviewed copy, and sends one intentional message.

5
Step 05 · Verify

Confirm the outcome

The workflow is not complete when the agent clicks Send. It is complete when the interface shows the new message in the correct conversation and confirms that it was sent.

Topic 03 · Human review

What remains human

Three things I do not delegate blindly.

01

Identity

I confirm the recipient whenever search results are ambiguous.

02

Tone

I review professional, sensitive, or relationship-dependent wording.

03

Scope

I use the workflow for intentional one-to-one communication, not bulk unsolicited outreach.

Output · Reusable template

What you can take away

A reusable prompt with an approval checkpoint.

Replace the bracketed fields and keep the identity check when the name is not unique.

Open LinkedIn and find [person’s name]. Confirm that the profile matches [company, role, school, or another identifying detail]. Draft this message: [message]. Show me the final version before sending.
