The operating model

The same five-part pattern sits behind both agents.

The agent can move quickly because the outcome and the boundaries are explicit.

01

Intent

Define the result, not just the topic.

02

Context

Provide the people, tools, evidence, and constraints.

03

Execution

Let the agent navigate, compare, and assemble.

04

Review

Pause at identity, privacy, or high-impact decisions.

05

Output

Verify the action or deliver a decision-ready artifact.

What stays human

Delegation works better when the review boundary is visible.

Identity

Confirm the right person.

Shared names, old profiles, and similar roles can make a confident action wrong.

Privacy

Show the workflow, not private data.

Names, emails, chat histories, and account identifiers are removed or abbreviated here.

Judgment

Separate evidence from inference.

The agent can find a pattern. A human still decides whether the evidence supports the conclusion.
