## 🤖 Selected AI Projects

### 💜 [LoveBridge — AI Relationship Coach](https://www.loverbridge.world/)

LoveBridge helps people make sense of relationship conflicts, communication breakdowns, and emotionally complicated decisions. I designed the product around guided reflection rather than one-shot chatbot answers: users bring a real situation, and the AI helps them clarify what happened, recognize the underlying pattern, and find a constructive next step. The goal is not to replace human judgment, but to make thoughtful, empathetic guidance easier to access when people need it.

### 🧭 PRD Review Agent — From Rough Idea to Clear Product Decision

Product ideas often move too quickly from an exciting concept to a build request. I created an agent that slows down that jump in a useful way. It reviews a draft PRD from three angles—user need, feasibility, and priority—then surfaces missing assumptions and questions for the team. The result is a more focused product conversation and a much faster path from early research to a prototype worth testing.

### ✨ AI Productivity Workflows — Documents, Slides, and Spreadsheets

I worked on AI experiences that turn a user's intent into usable work products across documents, presentations, spreadsheets, and Google Workspace. The interesting product challenge was not simply generating content; it was preserving structure, supporting iteration, and fitting AI into workflows people already understand. I explored how context, lightweight collaboration, and clear review points can make generated output feel dependable instead of disposable.

### 🔎 Explainable Transaction Risk Agent

I designed an AI-assisted review workflow for large transaction datasets where every flagged item needed a reason a human could inspect. The system combined structured checks with language-model analysis to surface unusual patterns, summarize why they mattered, and keep the reviewer in control. It was a practical lesson in building AI for high-trust environments: speed matters, but traceability and judgment matter more.

## 🧪 Machine Learning in Production

### 🎬 Production Movie Recommendation Service

Building a recommendation service for a Netflix-like platform with roughly one million users and a catalog of 20,000 movies. The project goes beyond choosing a model: it covers offline evaluation, cold-start behavior, low-latency inference, Kafka event streams, deployment, monitoring, drift, and the feedback loops created when recommendations influence future user behavior.

### 🧪 Model Release Gates with W&B and LLM Stress Testing

Evaluated competing sentiment models as a real ship-or-no-ship decision instead of relying on a single accuracy score. I used Weights & Biases to inspect behavioral slices and regressions, generated targeted edge cases with an LLM, and translated the findings into fixed automated quality and latency gates. The project reinforced an important production lesson: a model can look better on average and still be unsafe—or simply too slow—to release.

### 🐳 Containerized ML Training and Inference

Built a reproducible two-container pipeline that trains a classifier, persists the model in a shared Docker volume, and serves predictions through a Flask API. Docker Compose connects training and inference, while health checks, request validation, and host-mounted prediction logs make the service observable and easier to debug.

### 🧰 [Coding Agents & Agent Harnesses](https://github.com/lew2-wq/mlip-lab2-agent-harness)

Compared a coding agent's default workflow with an enhanced harness using Playwright, MCP, and reusable Skills. I used the agent's testing trace to improve the Skill itself, then reran end-to-end tests to see where better tooling helped and where human judgment was still essential.

### 🌊 [Kafka for Data Streaming](https://github.com/lew2-wq/mlip-kafka-lab)

Implemented producer and consumer workflows for a Kafka topic, explored offset management and replay from arbitrary points in the log, and used `kcat` to inspect and monitor the stream. The work connects messaging concepts to practical recovery, continuity, and real-time ML data pipelines.
